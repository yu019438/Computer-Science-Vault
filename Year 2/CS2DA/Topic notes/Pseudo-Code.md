#CS2DA 
- Pseudo-code is a method of communication between programmers that describes what a program/algorithm does
- Importantly, there is **no standardised pseudo-code language**.  It varies between applications but generally combines programming logical with natural English
- This makes it easier for readers to understand the nature of a program/code block without being proficient in the required language
